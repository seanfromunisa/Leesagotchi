# Leesagotchi
Basic small-window 'Soot Sprite' Tamagotchi for Leesa.

# How to play
- Petting the soot will also fill their happiness meter.
- Happiness is reduced by an amount relative to how hungry the soot is.
- Feeding the soot fills their stomach and happiness meters.
- Merge items to collect food rewards

# Screens
The game features three screens, corelating to the three circular buttons on the UI:
- Pet: The middle button, the mushroom, opens the main, soot caretaking screen.
- Merge: The right button, the colliding arrows, opens the merging minigame.
- Stats: The left button, the box, opens the food and statistic tracking screen.

# Pet
This screen features the soot sprite pet, and the stomach and happiness meters.
- Hold left mouse on the soot to pet them. Petting will frequently raise happiness.
- Click left mouse on the candy star to feed the soot. Feeding requires 10 food.

# Merge
This screen featues a random assortment of 12 items, always containing a pair.
- Click a level 1 or 2 item to highlight it. Click a matching item to merge.
- Click a level 3 item to redeem for food.

# Stats
This screen displays totals for time spent in-game, happiness raised, and items merged. Also shows the current food stockpile.

# Merge paths
The following is a guide to merge Leesa related items (Level 1 -> Level 2 -> Level 3)
- Mango -> Hungry Jack's Orange and Mango drink -> Mango Loco
- Controller -> Teddy bear kart -> Mushroom cup trophy
- Chicken -> Hoverboard -> DeLorean
- Stumpy Spark -> Carry Spark -> Hauling Spark
- White Cap mushroom -> Dragon -> Skyrim helmet
- Carrot -> Leaves -> Pickle Rick
- F1 tyre -> F1 helmet -> Charles LeClerc
- Paperclip -> Fountain pen -> Journal
